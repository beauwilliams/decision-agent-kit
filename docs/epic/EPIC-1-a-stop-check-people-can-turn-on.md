# 1 — A stop check people can turn on

## Goal

A person can turn the stop check on and trust what it flags: when a turn ends with unverified or unfinished work, a note says so, and it rarely speaks when nothing is wrong.

It matters because it is the first feature of the kit, and the one that has to earn the others their place (ADR-6).
